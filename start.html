What a standard door request includes

A door quote uses the same choices as the door builder. It is one door:

  • Door type: Storefront / Entrance, Hollow Metal / Steel, Fire-Rated Door, Security / Safe Room, Interior / Exterior Swing, or Sliding Barn Door.
  • Material: one of the materials that type already offers, or Recommend for me.
  • Size: Single door, 3' × 7' (36" × 84"), Single door, 3' × 8' (36" × 96"), Pair of doors, 6' × 7' (72" × 84"), a custom width and height in inches, or Not sure.
  • Hardware: at least one option the builder already lists for that door, or Recommend for me.
  • How many, if more than one of the same door. Where it goes, if you want to say.
  • Your name, and a phone number or an email. Company, project address or city, timeline, and notes are optional.

What happens next: the request goes to the office, and the office follows up. It is not an instant purchase. Partitions are not in this form. Call or email about those. More than one different door can be sent from the door builder on the homepage.
